Deck template 08 / NN

Section · Non-negotiables

Four key rules.

  • Rule 01

    A short imperative heading

    The body can run long here — this pattern absorbs formal, careful text better than cards do. Two to four lines per rule.

  • Rule 02

    Another heading

    Rows are separated by hairlines, with a heavier rule at the top of the ledger.

  • Rule 03

    A third heading

    Four rows fit comfortably; five if the bodies stay short.

  • Rule 04

    A heading that can even run quite long without breaking the shape

    If you're quoting policy, quote it verbatim and resist the urge to edit.
